Errors are announced to screen readers straight away (`role="alert"`); the other tones are announced politely (`role="status"`).

<Preview
  code={`
<Message tone="info">Info: something worth knowing.</Message>
<Message tone="success">Success: it worked.</Message>
<Message tone="warning">Warning: check this before you go on.</Message>
<Message tone="error">Error: something to fix.</Message>`}
>
  <div class="flex w-full flex-col gap-2">
    <Message tone="info">Info: something worth knowing.</Message>
    <Message tone="success">Success: it worked.</Message>
    <Message tone="warning">Warning: check this before you go on.</Message>
    <Message tone="error">Error: something to fix.</Message>
  </div>
</Preview>

Say what happened in the text too: the colour and edge are a cue, not the message.
