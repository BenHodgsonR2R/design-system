## Theme toggle

`ThemeToggle` cycles the system, light and dark themes. The choice is saved, and every toggle on the page stays in step, including the one in this site's header.

<Preview code={`<ThemeToggle />`}>
  <ThemeToggle align="start" client:load />
</Preview>

Call `applyStoredTheme()` before the first render, so the saved choice applies without a flash. To build your own control, use `storedTheme()` and `setTheme(choice)`.

## Tooltip

`Tooltip` shows a hint while its trigger is hovered or focused from the keyboard; Escape hides it. Screen readers don't hear it, so give the trigger a label that says the same.

<Preview
  code={`
<Tooltip content="Copies the link" align="start">
  <Button variant="secondary" aria-label="Copy link. Copies the link">
    Copy link
  </Button>
</Tooltip>`}
>

  </Tooltip>
</Preview>
