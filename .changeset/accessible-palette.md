Make every colour pair meet WCAG AA in light and dark mode, with a test suite that checks it. Add status colours (`danger`, `warning`, `success`, `info`), six colour-blind-safe categorical colours (`data-1` to `data-6`), a `secondary` accent, a `focus` colour and a `surface-sunken` well. Add `Badge` with `dataTone()`, and `ThemeToggle` with `applyStoredTheme()` to pick light, dark or the system theme; `Page` shows the toggle. `Message` gains `success` and `warning` tones.
