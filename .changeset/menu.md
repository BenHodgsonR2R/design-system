Adds `Menu`, a hamburger menu for headers that run out of room. It holds whatever it's given, styling links and marking the one with `aria-current="page"`. Its lines fold into a cross as it opens and what it holds drops in one after another; it closes on Escape, an outside click, tabbing out or choosing a link, and the arrow keys, Home and End move between what's inside.
