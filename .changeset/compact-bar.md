The page menu bar no longer shrinks in place. The full bar scrolls away with the page, and a compact bar slides down from the top, with a slight spring, once the full bar is out of view. A thin line along its bottom edge fills as the page scrolls, in browsers with scroll-driven animations. The menu bar caret blinks with hard on-off steps, like a real caret, and switches off after about five seconds instead of fading.
