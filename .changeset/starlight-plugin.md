First release. `designSystem()` styles a Starlight site with `@bhodgson/design-system`: its title, theme picker, hero, page title, content, pagination, footer and search. `loadApi()`, `apiSidebar()` and `apiPaths()` with `ApiPage.astro` document a package from its source with TypeDoc, with a page per export showing its props, fields or values, examples, and what uses it. `PagefindSearch` searches a Pagefind index with the design system's `Search`.
